# Acceptable Use Policy — Hireloom

> **DRAFT — review with counsel before launch.**

Hireloom exists to make job searches **better-targeted, not higher-volume.** Using it you agree:

## You will not
- **Spam.** No mass, low-fit, or auto-generated blast applications. Hireloom scores fit
  honestly and discourages low-fit applications by design; don't defeat that.
- **Misrepresent.** No fabricated experience, credentials, or metrics. Generated CVs and
  answers must draw only from your real record.
- **Abuse infrastructure.** No scraping at volume, no attempts to bypass rate limits or
  quotas, no probing internal/network resources via posting URLs (SSRF), no automated
  account creation.
- **Break the law** or any third party's terms (including job boards' and your AI provider's),
  or infringe others' rights.
- **Compromise security.** No reverse-engineering of access controls, no accessing other
  tenants' data, no uploading malware.

## Automation boundary
Hireloom drafts; **you** submit. Do not use Hireloom to operate unattended bots that submit
applications or impersonate you to employers. Respect recruiters' time — every application a
human reads costs attention.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in warning, suspension, or termination. Serious or illegal abuse may be
reported to the relevant authorities. Report abuse: _abuse@hireloom.app (TBD)_.

## AI transparency note (EU AI Act — limited-risk)
Hireloom uses AI to assist your job search. Outputs are AI-generated drafts that you review and
control; Hireloom makes no automated decision about any person and is not an employer screening
tool. Always verify AI output before relying on it.
