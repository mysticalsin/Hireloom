FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this just another auto-apply bot?+

No — and deliberately so. Hireloom drafts everything up to the submit button, then stops. You review and click send. We sell quality and fit, not volume, because mass-applying gets you ignored.

What does "bring your own key" mean?+

You connect your own AI provider key (Anthropic and OpenAI-compatible providers supported). You pay your provider directly at cost — we never mark up tokens. Your subscription is for the platform, not the inference.

Will it lie on my résumé to beat the ATS?+

Never. Truthful tailoring is the core principle. Hireloom rewrites and reweights from your real record only — no invented metrics, no skills you don't have. It sharpens; it doesn't fabricate.

Is my career data private?+

Yes. Your CV and pipeline are yours — encrypted at rest, never sold, exportable and deletable any time. The core is open-source, so you can also self-host and audit it.

Can I self-host instead of paying?+

Absolutely. The core is open-source and free forever under a bring-your-own-key model. The hosted plans add convenience, inbox signals, autopilot, and premium features.

What happens after I land the job?+

Cancel any time — no lock-in. Most people pause between searches and come back when they're hunting again. Your pipeline history is waiting when you return.
